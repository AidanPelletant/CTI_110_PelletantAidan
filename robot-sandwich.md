# \# Robot Sandwich Instructions



#### \## Assumptions

\* The robot is on, it's working, and is standing in front of the kitchen table.

\* The robot's hands are currently empty.

\* The items are sitting on the counter directly in front of the robot:

&#x20; \* One unopened bag of sliced bread, with a plastic twist clip.

&#x20; \* One unopened container of ketchup.

&#x20; \* One clean plate.

&#x20; \* One clean butter knife.



#### \## Instructions

1\. Extend right hand toward the plastic twist clip on the bread bag.

2\. Pinch the plastic twist clip with right-hand fingers and pull it off the bag.

3\. Place the plastic twist clip on the counter.

4\. Reach inside the bread bag with right hand and grab one slice of bread.

5\. Pull the slice of bread out of the bag and place it flat on the center of the plate.

6\. Reach inside the bread bag with right hand and grab a second slice of bread.

7\. Pull the second slice of bread out of the bag and place it flat on the counter next to the plate.

8\. Grab the container of ketchup container with left hand.

9\. Grasp the lid of the ketchup container with right hand.

10\. Twist the lid counterclockwise until it detaches from the container.

11\. Place the lid on the counter with the open side facing up.

12\. Release the body of the ketchup container with left hand.

13\. Pick up the butter knife by the handle using right hand.

14\. Insert the blade of the butter knife into the ketchup container until the tip is submerged in ketchup.

15\. Scoop approximately one tablespoon of ketchup onto the flat side of the knife blade.

16\. Remove the knife from the container.

17\. Hold the slice of bread on the plate with left hand.

18\. Wipe the blade of the knife across the top face of the bread slice on the plate until the ketchup is evenly spread.

19\. Place the butter knife down on the counter.

20\. Pick up the second slice of bread from the counter using right hand.

21\. Place the second slice of bread directly on top of the ketchup-covered slice on the plate.



#### \## Done When

\* Two slices of bread are stacked on top of each other on the plate with ketchup contained in between them, and the robot's hands are empty.
